Frequently asked questions

Learn about how ad credits work.

How do I claim an ad credit?

To claim an ad credit, complete the requirements listed in the offer before the offer expires. You’ll be able to find all your claimed ad credits here.

How do I use an ad credit?

Ad credits you’ve claimed will automatically apply to your ongoing ads and any new ads you create.

Do I have to spend money to use an ad credit?

Once your ad credit is applied, it will be charged before your primary payment method, no matter what budget you set. You’ll only be charged if the amount you spend on ads is greater than the value of your ad credit.
